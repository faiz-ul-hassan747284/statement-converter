Privacy Policy

Statement Converter is a personal tool operated by and for its sole user, the account owner.

With the owner's consent, it reads emails from the owner's own Gmail account that match bank-alert and purchase-receipt filters, extracts transaction details (date, amount, description) from them, and writes those details to a Google Sheet owned by the same person. It also applies a label to processed emails so they are not read twice.

No data is shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party. No data is stored anywhere other than the owner's own Google account and the owner's own server. The tool has no other users.

Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google account permissions page.
